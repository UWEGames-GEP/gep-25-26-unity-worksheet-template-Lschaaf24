My main scene is in Assets -> Scenes not in GEP Core.
My current projects features an inventory sytem based on item icons. To pick up an item off the ground you have to look at the item and press E when the prompt appears adding the item straight into your inventory. You can press Esc to open the player inventory and pause the game. You can drag items out of your inventory and they will spawn the prefab and allow you to pick it up again. There is also weapon racks in the building that can be accessed and then take items from by dragging from the rack inventory to the player inventory. Most of this was done using scriptable item object and many classes to allow for different behaviors.
